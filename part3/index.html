Chapter 3A

General

Foundational provisions for all Part 3 markings: standardization of meaning, materials, the six standard colors, longitudinal-line width/pattern vocabulary, and minimum retroreflectivity maintenance, plus the master marking detail sheets (Figures 3A-101(CA) through 3A-114(CA)).

Chapter 3B

Pavement and Curb Markings

The core Part 3 marking chapter: center lines, lane lines, edge lines, no-passing zones, reversible-lane and two-way left-turn markings, channelizing lines, raised pavement markers, curb markings, stop/yield lines, word/symbol/arrow markings, chevron/diagonal markings, parking space markings, and speed reduction/speed hump markings.

Chapter 3C

Crosswalk Markings

Design and application requirements for transverse-line and high-visibility (longitudinal bar, ladder, bar pair) crosswalk markings, uncontrolled-crossing screening criteria, and treatments at roundabouts, diverging diamond interchanges, and refuge islands.

12 sections
Chapter 3D

Circular Intersection Markings

Pavement marking design for roundabouts, traffic circles, and rotaries, covering lane lines, edge lines, yield lines, word/symbol markings, and lane-use arrows.

7 sections
Chapter 3E

Preferential Lane Markings for Motor Vehicles

Pavement marking design for HOV, toll, priced-managed, transit, and other preferential lanes, including barrier-, buffer-, and contiguous-separated and counter-flow configurations, word/symbol markings, and part-time shoulder travel.

4 sections
Chapter 3F

Markings for Toll Plazas

Longitudinal lane, purple ETC-supplement, and pavement word/symbol marking requirements that channelize toll-plaza approaches by payment method and separate open-road tolling lanes.

3 sections
Chapter 3G

Delineators

Design classes, colors, application criteria, and placement/spacing formulas for retroreflective delineators, plus California-specific details for emergency passageways, narrow bridges, runaway truck ramps, and median barriers.

7 sections
Chapter 3H

Colored Pavement

Rules distinguishing aesthetic surface treatments from colored pavement used as a traffic control device, and the color-specific applications for yellow, white, green, red, and purple colored pavement.

8 sections
Chapter 3I

Channelizing Devices Used for Emphasis of Pavement Marking Patterns

Requirements for channelizers and tubular markers used to add physical emphasis to pavement markings, including channelizer design, color, and spacing, and tubular marker placement at unsignalized crosswalks.

Chapter 3J

Marking and Delineation of Islands and Sidewalk Extensions

Standards and guidance for marking traffic islands and median islands, including approach-end treatments, channelizing lines, curb markings, and delineation, plus criteria for designating sidewalk extensions with pavement markings.

7 sections
Chapter 3K

Rumble Strip Markings

Provisions for pavement markings used in combination with longitudinal and transverse rumble strips, including rumble stripe coloring and transverse rumble strip color requirements.
